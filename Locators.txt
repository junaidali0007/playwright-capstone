In Selenium you used By.Id, By.XPath, and By.CssSelector. Playwright prefers locators that match how users see the page. Use them in this order:

getByRole: buttons, links, headings, checkboxes (best default)
getByLabel: form fields with labels
getByPlaceholder: inputs with placeholder text
getByText: visible text
getByTestId: data-testid attributes (great when the dev team adds them)
locator('css=...') or XPath: last resort
